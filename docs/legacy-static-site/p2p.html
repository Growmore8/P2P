FAQ

P2P questions, answered

What is P2P USDT trading?

Peer-to-peer (P2P) means you transact directly with a counterparty — in this case, SRM Fintech Solutions' merchant desk — instead of placing orders on an exchange order book. You pay INR and receive USDT, or send USDT and receive INR.

How is the rate decided?

Our desk sets buy and sell rates based on prevailing market conditions. The rate shown in the calculator is indicative and is locked when our desk confirms your order on WhatsApp.

How long does an order take?

Most orders complete within the stated average completion time after payment (or USDT) is received and KYC is verified. First-time customers may take longer due to verification.

Why is TDS deducted on sell orders?

Under Section 194S of the Income-tax Act, 1% TDS may apply on the transfer of virtual digital assets. Where applicable, we deduct it and it is reflected in your quote. Please consult a tax professional for your specific situation.

Can someone else pay on my behalf?

No. Payments and payouts must be from / to a bank account or UPI ID in the verified customer's own name. Third-party transactions will be refunded to source and the order cancelled.

What if I sent USDT on the wrong network?

Transactions on the wrong network may be permanently lost. Always confirm the network with our desk before sending. Contact us immediately if a mistake occurs and we will try to help, but recovery cannot be guaranteed.

Is SRM Fintech Solutions an exchange?

No. SRM Fintech Solutions is a sole proprietorship offering eligible digital asset transaction services. We do not claim to be an RBI-authorised bank, stock exchange, cryptocurrency exchange or investment adviser.
